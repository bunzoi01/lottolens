How to read this

"Random picks" means {{ result.random_trials }} independent sets of 10 random games per draw. Their spread shows how much luck alone moves results over this many draws. If LottoLens sits inside the shaded band, it is doing what any set of numbers would do. That's the expected outcome: every 6-number combination has the same 1 in 8,145,060 chance of Division 1 in every draw, whatever happened before.

"Expected" is the exact long-run figure from the game's odds (about 1 prize-winning game in {{ '%.0f'|format(1 / t.prize_rate) }}).
